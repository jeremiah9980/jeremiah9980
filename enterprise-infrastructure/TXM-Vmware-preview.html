Headline findings

  • AUS vCenter footprint: 838 total VMs (768 powered on) across 40 ESXi hosts.
  • DR vCenter footprint: 92 total VMs (77 powered on) across 16 ESXi hosts.
  • Primary Cisco hypervisor platform is UCSB-B200-M5. The UCS physical export also includes 20 standalone UCSC-C240-M5L nodes in the Cohesity organization that do not appear as ESXi hosts in the RVTools exports.
  • AUS includes an additional Dell VRTX footprint: 8 PowerEdge M640 hosts spread across 4 small clusters.
  • Cisco host network pattern is consistent around MGMT / VMData / vMotion dvSwitches, with DMZ dvSwitches on the main Cluster_1 and Telecom stacks.
  • Cisco storage pathing is Fibre Channel-based (Cisco UCS VIC Fnic / FCoE controllers). Dell VRTX hosts use Shared PERC 8 Mini internal/shared storage rather than FC HBAs.
  • AUS RVTools health output flags all 40 AUS host certificates as expiring; DR certificates are reported good.
